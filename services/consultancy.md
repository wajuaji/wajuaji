## You do not need to speak tech to work with us

A lot of founders come to us knowing exactly what they want to build but having no idea how to get there technically. That gap can feel uncomfortable, especially when you are talking to developers who throw around technical terms without stopping to explain what any of it means for you.

We do not do that. When we sit down with you, we talk about your product and what you are trying to solve. The technical side is our job to figure out and explain back to you in a way that makes sense.

## What this looks like in practice

Sometimes you just need someone to look at what you have and tell you honestly whether it is heading in the right direction. Other times you need help scoping a product from scratch so you know what you are committing to before any money is spent. We have done both.

A consultancy engagement with us might cover:

- Mapping out what needs to be built and in what order
- Reviewing an existing system to identify what is working and what is not
- Helping you evaluate a proposal or quote from another developer
- Planning a realistic timeline and budget for your product

## How we start

Every consultancy engagement begins with a free discovery call. There is no commitment attached to it. We use that time to understand where you are and what you are trying to build.

If it makes sense to work together beyond that, we will tell you. If it does not, we will tell you that too.
