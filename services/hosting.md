## Nobody warns you about this part

You spend time and money getting your product built and launched, and then quietly in the background, there is a whole other job that never really ends. Servers need watching. Domains need renewing. SSL certificates expire. Traffic spikes at the worst possible moment and everything grinds to a halt. And when your site goes down, you are usually the last one to find out.

Most business owners are not supposed to know how any of that works. But without someone watching it, it becomes your problem anyway.

## What it looks like when we handle it

We set up your hosting infrastructure properly from the start and keep an eye on it after. Your domain is configured, your site is secured, and monitoring is in place so issues get caught early. If something goes wrong, we deal with it before it turns into a full outage.

You get to focus on running your business. We handle the part that keeps it online.

## How the plan works

Managed hosting is completely optional and is offered as an annual plan at an affordable and reasonable rate. It is not a complicated arrangement. We agree on what is covered, you know what you are paying, and everything else is handled on our end.

If you are interested in taking hosting off your hands entirely, reach out via email, phone, or WhatsApp and we will walk you through what the plan covers.
