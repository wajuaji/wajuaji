## The problem with doing it by hand

If you are running a business that accepts M-Pesa payments, you already know the drill. A customer pays, you get an SMS, you check it, you update a spreadsheet or confirm an order manually, and then you move on to the next one. It works, until it does not. Payments get missed, orders slip through, and a chunk of your day disappears into admin work that should not exist.

A proper payment integration takes all of that off your plate. When a customer pays, your system knows immediately and responds on its own.

## What we set up for you

We connect your website, app, or internal tools directly to M-Pesa and other payment channels so that money moves and your system keeps up in real time. Depending on what your business needs, this might include STK push prompts that make it easy for customers to pay, automatic payment confirmation and order updates, a dashboard where you can monitor transactions at a glance, or connectivity to other payment gateways beyond M-Pesa.

## How we approach it

Before we write a single line of code, we sit with you to understand how money moves through your business right now. That shapes everything. We build around your existing workflow rather than asking you to change how you operate.

Payment systems do not leave room for guesswork, so every integration is tested thoroughly before it goes anywhere near your customers.
