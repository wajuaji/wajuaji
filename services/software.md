## What custom software means

Most people hear "custom software" and picture something complicated and expensive. In reality, it just means software that is built around you rather than the other way around.

Off-the-shelf tools are built for everyone, which means they are a perfect fit for no one. You end up bending your workflow to fit the tool instead of the tool fitting your workflow. Custom software fixes that. Everything is shaped around how you operate, not how a product manager at some company imagined you might.

## What we build

We have built e-commerce stores, learning platforms, marketing websites, and personal blogs, and each one looked different because each client was different. Some projects are simple and done in a couple of weeks. Others have several moving parts and take longer to get right. Either way, we figure it out together and build it properly.

To give you a sense of what falls under this service:

- Websites and web applications built from the ground up
- WordPress builds, customisations, and migrations
- REST APIs that connect your systems to external services
- Payment integrations including M-Pesa and card payments

## How we work with you

We do not start with code. We start with a conversation.

Before anything is built, we sit down with you to understand what you are trying to build and why it matters. Then we map out a clear scope and timeline so nothing comes as a surprise later.

From there, we check in with you regularly as the work progresses. If something feels off or you want to pull the direction a little, that conversation happens early before it turns into a bigger problem. You will never get to the end and feel like a stranger to your own product. That is something we take personally.

## Ready to talk about your project?

If you have something in mind, even if it is still taking shape, we are happy to hear it. Reach out via email, phone, or WhatsApp and we will take it from there.
