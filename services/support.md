## Launching is just the beginning

A lot of people think that once a product goes live, the hard part is over. In some ways it is, but a live product is also a product that is being used, tested by real people, and exposed to the kind of wear that only happens in the real world.

Things change. Browsers update. Plugins fall out of date. A piece of code that worked fine six months ago quietly stops working. Security gaps appear in software you did not even know needed patching. None of this is dramatic on its own, but left unattended it builds up into something that is.

## What we take care of

We keep your product in good shape after launch. That means fixing bugs when they come up, applying updates before they become security risks, and keeping an eye on performance so small slowdowns do not turn into bigger ones. If you want a new feature added or something tweaked, that falls under this too.

## How we work with you on this

We start by looking at the current state of your product so we understand what we are stepping into. From there we agree on a support arrangement that fits how much attention your system needs. Some products need regular check-ins, others just need someone available when something comes up. We are flexible on how that looks.

If your product was built by someone else, we are open to taking it on. We will review it first and give you an honest picture of what we are dealing with before committing to anything.

## Want to talk about keeping your product healthy?

Reach out via email, phone, or WhatsApp and we will figure out what kind of support makes sense for where you are.
